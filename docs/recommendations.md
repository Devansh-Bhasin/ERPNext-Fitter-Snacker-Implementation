# ERP Recommendations

## Recommended ERP Platform
ERPNext is recommended as the preferred ERP platform for Fitter Snacker.

---

## Reasons for Recommendation

### Cost Effectiveness
ERPNext is fully open-source and minimizes long-term licensing expenses.

### Manufacturing Support
The platform provides strong support for:
- Production planning
- Inventory tracking
- Bills of Materials
- Warehouse operations

### Centralized Data Management
ERPNext integrates:
- Accounting
- Sales
- Manufacturing
- Inventory
- Supply chain operations

within a centralized database environment.

### Scalability
The platform can scale alongside organizational growth and evolving business requirements.

---

## Security Recommendations
- Implement role-based access controls
- Enable regular backups
- Apply software updates regularly
- Monitor user access logs
- Enforce strong authentication policies

---

## Long-Term Benefits
ERPNext implementation can help:
- Improve operational efficiency
- Reduce manual workflows
- Improve decision-making
- Increase reporting accuracy
- Support business scalability
