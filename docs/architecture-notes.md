# ERP Architecture Notes

## Proposed ERP Environment
The proposed ERP environment is based on ERPNext deployed within a centralized web-based infrastructure.

---

## Core Modules
- Accounting
- Sales
- Inventory Management
- Manufacturing
- Human Resources
- Supply Chain Management

---

## Deployment Options
ERPNext can be deployed:
- On-premises
- Within cloud infrastructure
- On Linux-based server environments

---

## Benefits of Centralized ERP
- Shared organizational database
- Real-time operational reporting
- Reduced data duplication
- Improved visibility across departments
- Better coordination between business processes

---

## Business Objectives
The ERP implementation aims to:
- Reduce operational inefficiencies
- Improve inventory forecasting
- Minimize reporting delays
- Improve manufacturing coordination
- Support future organizational growth
