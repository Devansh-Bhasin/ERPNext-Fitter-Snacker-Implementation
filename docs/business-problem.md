
# Business Problem Analysis

## Company Background
Fitter Snacker is a manufacturing company currently using separate systems for accounting, inventory management, sales, and human resources.

As the business expanded, the disconnected system architecture created operational inefficiencies and inconsistent data management across departments.

---

## Current Operational Challenges

### Data Duplication
Employees are required to manually enter the same information into multiple systems, increasing the likelihood of inconsistencies and errors.

### Order Processing Errors
Approximately 17% of customer orders contain errors due to manual workflows and lack of centralized data synchronization.

### Delivery Delays
Around 20% of customer deliveries are delayed because inventory and production systems are not updated in real time.

### Inventory Management Issues
Inventory levels are estimated to be approximately 20% higher than necessary because of inaccurate forecasting and disconnected reporting systems.

### Financial Reporting Delays
The accounting department requires approximately 10 days to complete monthly reconciliation processes due to fragmented financial data.

---

## Business Impact
These operational inefficiencies negatively impact:
- Customer satisfaction
- Supply chain coordination
- Inventory visibility
- Financial reporting
- Decision-making processes
- Operational efficiency

---

## Need for ERP System
An integrated ERP solution is required to:
- Centralize organizational data
- Improve operational visibility
- Reduce manual data entry
- Improve reporting accuracy
- Streamline manufacturing workflows
- Support future business growth
